Keep the good stuff.

NOOG is ready to filter accessible websites. Pornographic-image checks and gambling-promotion rules start on by default. No account or API key required.

Your device does the checking

The bundled model checks images and readable video frames locally. Caption rules look for promotional phrases and your custom keywords. There is no external classification service or separate AI installation.

Private spaces stay private

Known private apps, supported private-message routes, forms and editable content are skipped. You can pause any other site from the toolbar. Chrome’s protected pages and embedded frames are outside automatic coverage.

You’re in control

Swaps are reversible. “Show once” reveals an item; “Keep this post” remembers a correction locally. Pick cats, nature, wisdom or quiet replacements in the NOOG toolbar.

Unchecked media stays visible by default. Detection is imperfect: ordinary art, health or fitness images can be misclassified, and video samples can miss content between frames.
