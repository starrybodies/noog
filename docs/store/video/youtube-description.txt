NOOG — Reverse the Goon. Your attention. Your rules.

A free experimental Chrome extension that covers unwanted images and promotional posts with cats, nature, wisdom or quiet cards. Bundled on-device image checks and local caption rules. No account, API key or separate AI installation required.

NOOG is pronounced Nuu-g, with a hard g.

Version 0.10.0 was submitted for Chrome Web Store review on October 14, 2026. Store installation is coming after approval. Detection and website coverage are imperfect; swaps are reversible. Known private apps, supported private-message routes, forms and editors are excluded. Pause unfamiliar private sites in the toolbar.
